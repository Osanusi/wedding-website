# Party Theme Songs

Drop MP3 files here for each wedding party member.
When hovering over their spotlight card, a DJ scratch plays
and their custom theme song starts.

## Expected files (match the `themeSong` paths in Home.tsx):
- `sarah.mp3` — Maid of Honor entrance song
- `mike.mp3` — Best Man entrance song

Add more by updating the `partyMembers` array in `src/pages/Home.tsx`.

## DJ Scratch Sound
Also place a `dj-scratch.mp3` in `public/audio/` (parent folder).
A short 0.3-1s vinyl scratch sound effect works best.

### Free sources:
- [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/scratch/)
- [Freesound](https://freesound.org/search/?q=dj+scratch)
